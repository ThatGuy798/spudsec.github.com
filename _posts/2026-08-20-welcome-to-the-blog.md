This is the first post. Delete this once you've got real content going.

To add a new post, drop a file in `_posts/` named like:

```
YYYY-MM-DD-your-title-here.md
```

With front matter at the top:

```yaml
---
title: "Your Post Title"
tags: [security, potatoes]
---
```

Everything below the front matter is the post body, written in Markdown. It'll automatically show up on the `/blog/` listing, newest first.
